# Product Context

## Problem Statement
Musicians and content creators need a reliable, high-quality source for instrumentals (beats) that offers a modern and seamless purchasing experience. Producers need a platform that showcases their work effectively and facilitates sales without friction.

## Solution
Chromatik offers a specialized marketplace for beats with a focus on:
- **Visual & Audio Quality**: Presenting beats in an engaging way (likely waveforms, sleek players).
- **Ease of Use**: Streamlined browsing, filtering, and purchasing.
- **Creator Tools**: Efficient management of catalogs and sales tracking.

## User Experience Goals
- **"Wowed" First Impression**: The UI should be vibrant (chromatic theme), fast, and responsive.
- **Intuitive Discovery**: Easy to find beats by genre, mood, key, or BPM.
- **Secure & Trustworthy**: Clear licensing terms and secure checkout.
